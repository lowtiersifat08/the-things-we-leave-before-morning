"The first time Elias fell in love with Mara, she was buying flowers for a dead man. The second time, she was buying them for him."

In the sinking seaside city of Veyr, a lonely clockmaker meets a woman arguing with a bouquet at a railway station that no longer sees trains — and loses her, seven years later, to a note that reads "For when he forgets."
